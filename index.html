About Me

I'm a UI/UX Designer and Frontend Software Engineer who believes that great design is invisible — it just works. With nearly 5 years of experience, I specialize in creating beautiful, intuitive interfaces that solve real user problems.

Currently at Drivetech Intelligence Pvt Ltd, I lead UI/UX design initiatives and develop scalable frontend solutions. I bridge the gap between design and development, ensuring pixel-perfect implementations that maintain design integrity.

Previously at Invictus Web Solutions, I honed my skills in responsive web design, SEO optimization, and creating user-centered digital experiences. My design toolkit includes Figma for wireframing, prototyping, and creating design systems.

My design philosophy: Every pixel has a purpose, every interaction tells a story. I'm passionate about creating accessible, inclusive designs that delight users and drive business results.

My Design Process

Empathize

Research & understand user needs, pain points, and behaviors through interviews and analytics.

Define

Analyze findings to define clear problem statements and user personas that guide design decisions.

Ideate

Brainstorm solutions, sketch wireframes, and create low-fidelity prototypes for rapid iteration.

Prototype

Build high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma for user testing and stakeholder feedback.

Implement

Translate designs into production-ready code with pixel-perfect precision and smooth interactions.

Iterate

Test, gather feedback, and continuously improve based on real user data and analytics.
